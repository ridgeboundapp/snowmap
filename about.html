About Snow Lookout

The Why

If you've ever planned a mountain trip either on the shoulder seasons or during the winter, and you tried using the USFS Snow Depth map, you will have probably felt the same pain I've felt so many times (too many times). I hope you will find Snow Lookout useful. If you have any ides of how I can improve the tool, don't hesitate to rech out!

The What

Snow Lookout shows current snow depth across the mountains on an interactive map, so you can check conditions before you head out. Move your cursor over any spot to see the snow depth in inches (or feet for deep snow) along with the elevation at that exact location. Search for a town, trail or peak to jump straight to it, and switch between topographic, street and satellite maps to see the terrain you're planning for.

Snow depth comes from NOAA's fantastic snow data. Because it's modeled on a one square kilometer grid, treat it as a planning guide, not a measurement at a precise spot. Always check local conditions and avalanche forecasts before you go. Stay safe and have fun!
